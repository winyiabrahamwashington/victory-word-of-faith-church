Welcome to Our Family

Founded in the early 1990s by Pastor Dorothy Binwe Namahe, Victory Word of Faith Church Nabbingo International (VWFCI) began as a small living‑room fellowship and has grown into a thriving international ministry. We are a diverse community united by faith in Jesus Christ.

" For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith." – 1 John 5:4

From humble beginnings, Pastor Dorothy faithfully pursued God's call, and in 2005, the ministry was blessed with land dedicated for the church. Today, VWFCI continues to flourish, bringing people into the saving grace of our Lord Jesus Christ. Our mission is to equip believers with God's Word and send them out to impact communities through faith, prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit.
