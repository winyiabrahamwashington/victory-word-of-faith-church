Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about visiting, connecting, and getting involved with our church.

What should I expect on my first visit?

You'll be warmly welcomed by our hospitality team. Services include passionate worship (in English and Luganda), practical Bible teaching, and prayer ministry. Dress is casual to business casual - come as you are!

Do you have programs for children and youth?

Yes! We have Victory Kids Ministry for ages 3-12 during all Sunday services. Youth Explosion Ministry meets Sundays at 3:00 PM for ages 13-25. All children's and youth workers are trained and background-checked for safety.

How can I get involved in ministry?

We have over 15 different ministries you can join! Visit our Ministries page to see them all. Check our contact page and reach out to us for more information.

Do you offer counseling services?

Yes, we offer biblical counseling for individuals, couples, and families. Counseling is by appointment only and is completely confidential. Contact our counseling department at pastordorothy2002@yahoo.co.uk or call +256 706 098 195 to schedule.

Is the church accessible for people with disabilities?

Yes, our facilities are wheelchair accessible with ramps, accessible restrooms, and reserved parking. Please contact us in advance if you need special accommodations.
