Founder & Senior Pastor

Pastor Dorothy Binwe Namahe Namahe

Senior Pastor & Visionary Leader

Pastor Dorothy Binwe Namahe Namahe, the founder and shepherd of Victory Word of Faith Church Nabbingo International, is a living testimony of God's faithfulness and transformative power. Born in Kitoba Village, Hoima District, Uganda, her early life was marked by hardship and loss.

At just four years old, she lost her grandmother, her only caretaker. Left in the care of others who were unable or unwilling to nurture her, Dorothy faced neglect, hardship, and the heavy responsibility of caring for her younger siblings at an age when most children rely on adults for guidance.

Early Life & Education

Despite these challenges, Dorothy's determination and faith in God never wavered. Through sheer perseverance, she pursued education, studying part-time at Kololo High School in Kampala and later enrolling in a catering course at the Young Women Christian Association (YWCA) in 1984.
Even as she navigated the responsibilities of early parenthood and worked odd jobs—including co-founding a beauty salon with her husband, Charles Binwe—Dorothy's heart was increasingly drawn to God.

In 1992, a pivotal moment changed her life forever. After suffering for years from a debilitating chest ailment, she attended a prayer and fasting session at a local church. During an evening of prayer, she experienced a sudden, miraculous healing. The pain that had tormented her for years vanished instantly. Overcome with gratitude and awe, she ran to the pastor leading the prayer, repented her sins, and embraced her calling as a born-again Christian. That divine encounter ignited a deep passion for ministry that could not be ignored.

Guided by God, Pastor Dorothy began ministering in her local community, confronting the spiritual and moral challenges around her—including witchcraft, false religions, drunkenness, and immorality. She started humbly, sharing the gospel door to door, before hosting gatherings in her own home. Her commitment, faith, and courage laid the foundation for the birth of Victory Word of Faith Church Nabbingo International—a ministry born out of prayer, perseverance, and the relentless pursuit of God's vision.

"My passion is to see every believer walk in the fullness of their God-given destiny, equipped with the Word, empowered by the Spirit, and effective in their calling."

Today, under Pastor Dorothy's leadership, Victory Word of Faith Church stands as a beacon of hope and spiritual transformation. Her story—marked by struggle, miraculous healing, and unwavering faith—is a powerful reminder that no circumstance is too difficult for God to turn into victory. Through her ministry, countless lives continue to be touched, empowered, and restored, locally and internationally.

Our Leadership Philosophy

Servant Leadership

We follow Jesus' example of washing feet - leading by serving, not by lording over. Every leader is first a servant.

Team Ministry

We believe in collaborative leadership where each member's gifts complement others for effective ministry.

Multiplication

We invest in raising new leaders, empowering the next generation to continue the work of ministry.

Become a Leader

God is raising leaders in every generation. If you feel called to leadership or want to develop your leadership gifts, we have training and mentoring opportunities available.
